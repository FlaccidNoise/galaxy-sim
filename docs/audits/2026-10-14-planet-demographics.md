## Open questions
- **Row 8:** I did not confirm the Kepler period-ratio median and mode numbers. The Fabrycky+2014 and Weiss+2018 abstracts give only qualitative statements.
- **Row 11:** I did not confirm the frost-line exponent for low-mass stars. KK08 says the dependence is "weak" but gives no formula in its abstract.
- **Row 6:** Bryson+2021 eta-Earth comes from news coverage, not the paper.
- **Row 1:** The engulfment survival radius for stars other than 1 M☉ depends on the stellar auditor's giant-phase radius.
