    05About

    At McMaster I simulated a galaxy in Fortran 90 and watched a star cluster form its own spiral arms. This is the same idea again, in a browser tab, where you can turn it over.

    Every star pulls on every other. A Barnes-Hut tree groups the far ones, and the graphics card does the work through WebGPU. About 30% of the disc is gas that cools, settles thin, and turns into new stars where it gets dense. The heaviest stars end as supernovae and hand their matter back to the gas.

    Pick a star and keep zooming, and the view crosses into that star's own solar system. Pick a planet there to orbit it.
